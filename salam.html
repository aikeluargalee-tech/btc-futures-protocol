Beginner Glossary — 6 Terms In 30 Seconds Plain Definitions
Spot vs Futures: Spot is buying real Bitcoin directly. Futures is betting on price with borrowed money (faster moves and sharp reversals).
Floor & Ceiling: A floor (support) is where drops previously bounced. A ceiling (resistance) is where rallies previously stalled.
Sweep (Poke & Bounce): Price briefly pokes past a floor or ceiling and closes back inside — trapping late traders.
Funding Rate: Small fee between futures buyers and sellers. Positive means more bets on up moves; negative means more bets down.
Liquidations: Forced closes when leveraged traders (10x–100x) run out of margin. Clusters act like short-term magnets.
Why "Wait" Is Common: Most hours have no trigger. Waiting protects you from trading noise in the middle of a range.
