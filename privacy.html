1. OVERVIEW & SCOPE

This Privacy Policy applies to the BTC Short-Term Futures Protocol v4.0 mobile application (Android and iOS) and its companion web dashboard hosted at https://aikeluargalee-tech.github.io/btc-futures-protocol/.

This software is strictly a read-only market telemetry monitor, 4H regime viewer, position-sizing calculator, and AI prompt formatter. It does not require user registration, does not connect to brokerage or exchange API keys, and does not execute real-money trades or custody funds.

2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • No account registration, name, email address, phone number, or KYC identity documents.
  • No exchange API keys, wallet seed phrases, payment card details, or financial transaction histories.
  • No precise GPS location, contacts, microphone, or camera access.

3. TELEMETRY, NETWORK REQUESTS & CLIPBOARD USAGE

  • Market Telemetry: The app fetches public, non-personalized BTC market snapshots (data/state.json) and public spot price tickers directly over HTTPS.
  • Local Video Asset: The briefing clip (vGUZF.mp4) is bundled inside the application package and plays locally without tracking pixels.
  • Clipboard Export: Tapping [COPY AI ANALYSIS PROMPT + JSON] or [COPY RAW JSON ONLY] writes formatted market telemetry solely to your device clipboard on demand. Nothing is transmitted externally unless you paste it into an AI assistant of your choice.

4. ADVERTISING & GOOGLE ADMOB (MOBILE APP)

The Android and iOS mobile applications display Banner and Native Advanced advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Depending on your region and device consent settings, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (Android Advertising ID / AD_ID, or Apple IDFA if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).
  • Device metadata, IP-derived approximate region, app diagnostics, ad impressions, and interaction events for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.

Review Google's advertising privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or limit personalized advertising anytime in Android Settings (“Ads”) or iOS Settings (“Privacy & Security → Tracking”).

All signals, regime scores, risk calculations, and paper-execution metrics are provided solely for informational and educational purposes. Nothing displayed in the app constitutes investment advice or a solicitation to buy or sell any financial instrument.
