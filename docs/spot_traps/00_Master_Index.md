# The Spot Trader's Field Guide to Market Traps — Master Index

Welcome to **The Spot Trader's Field Guide to Market Traps**, a complete 16-part educational reference library engineered specifically for **unleveraged 1.0x Bitcoin spot swing traders** (buying BTC with USDT or defensively trimming BTC to USDT—zero leverage, margin, perpetuals, or futures advice).

Bitcoin trades 24/7 with massive perpetual-futures leverage stacked on top of relatively thinner spot order books, while institutional spot ETF and CME liquidity only operate on traditional weekday clocks. Because of that hybrid market structure, the exact same recurring chart, liquidity, derivatives, calendar, and behavioral traps repeat across every market cycle. Rather than chasing complex indicators, this library trains you to eliminate unforced errors by reading **flow permission, calendar liquidity, location, liquidity clearance, and completed candle closes** before committing tactical cash.

---

## Core Chain of Command

Every article in this series enforces one strict analytical hierarchy. Authority always flows **downward**—lower timeframes, visual order-book noise, weekend breakouts, and lagging indicators never overrule higher timeframes or settled institutional capital flows:

> **Phase 1 Macro, Calendar & Settled ETF Flows (🔴 RED / 🟡 AMBER / 🟢 GREEN) → Derivatives Positioning Hygiene (BTC-Unit OI & Funding Tails) → Daily (1D) Trend → 4-Hour (4H) Structure & Liquidity Clearance → 1-Hour (1H) Execution Trigger**

### Non-Negotiable 1.0x Spot Guardrails
1. **Separate Core Holdings from Tactical Cash:** Never panic-sell long-term core Bitcoin on intraday volatility, and never rush tactical cash into a 🔴 RED macro regime just because price looks "cheap."
2. **Candle Closes Over Wicks:** Intraday wicks through key levels are noise or liquidity sweeps; structural shifts require completed 1H/4H/1D candle closes.
3. **Post-Sweep Discipline:** Let nearby unswept liquidation pools clear first, then act on confirmed closed-candle reclaims or retests.
4. **Executed Flow Over Displayed Intent:** Liquidation heatmaps are modeled estimates and live L2 order-book walls can vanish in milliseconds—only executed volume and settled flows prove real demand.
5. **Native-Unit Derivatives Hygiene:** Always verify Open Interest in **BTC units** (not USD notional), judge funding only at true tail extremes outside the `+0.01%/8h` clamp, and remember that real spot ETF selling requires real spot buyers regardless of seller motive.
6. **Respect Session & Event Liquidity:** Discount weekend/holiday thin-book breakouts and stand completely aside during the initial 15-minute headline whipsaw around Tier-1 macro prints (CPI, PCE, FOMC) and major options expiries.
7. **Preserve Dry Powder as an Active Position:** Missing a vertical move costs $0; trapping tactical USDT underwater creates capital paralysis and ruins your next three decisions.

## How Humans, AI Models, and Machines Use This Archive

Every article in this folder is formatted in **Markdown (`.md`) with YAML frontmatter** so one file serves three consumers cleanly:

1. **Human Readers:** Clean headings, worked 1.0x spot math, side-by-side **Illusion vs. Structure** tables, and step-by-step **Anti-Trap Protocols** render natively in Obsidian, GitHub, mobile Markdown viewers, and browsers.
2. **AI Assistants & Knowledge Bases (e.g., NotebookLM, RAG agents):** Semantic headings and plain-English rules maximize retrieval accuracy without PDF extraction corruption.
3. **Automated Scripts & Agents:** Each file begins with a standardized YAML metadata header containing:
   - `id`: Unique machine key (`trap_01` through `trap_16`)
   - `title`: Full human-readable title
   - `pillar`: Series grouping
   - `mindset`: Locked to `1.0x_spot_only`
   - `regime_risk`: Traffic-light regimes (`RED`, `AMBER`, `GREEN`) where the trap is most dangerous
   - `phase_link`: Which workflow stage catches the trap (`Phase 1`, `Phase 2`, or both)
   - `related_traps`: Cross-references for graph linking and agent traversal
   - `tags`: Filterable keywords
